# Owen Teng · 滕新阳

**Co-founder, Robinson AI Lab · Management practitioner and AI product builder**

[简体中文](https://github.com/Robinson-AI-Lab/.github/blob/main/people/owen-teng.zh-CN.md) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Owen-Teng) · [Robinson AI Lab](https://github.com/Robinson-AI-Lab)

Owen works at the intersection of organizational management, AI, and gamification. Drawing on experience in human resources, organizational learning, and product development, he explores how real work can inform better AI tools—and how those tools can change the way people learn, make decisions, and collaborate.

His work connects three interests: **using data to understand organizational problems, using thoughtful interaction and gamification to support participation, and using AI to turn knowledge and experience into practical support for everyday work.** His experience spans gamified learning, AI digital employees, and personal knowledge-management tools.

At Robinson AI Lab, Owen brings a management practitioner's perspective to AI research and product development. He helps shape **FridayOS**, the lab's flagship initiative for a more approachable, human-centered Agent OS, and contributes to its product experience and front-end exploration. The aim is to help people understand what an agent is doing, delegate work clearly, and step in when judgment is needed.

## Research and practice interests

- AI-enabled management and organizational learning.
- Human–AI collaboration, with clear responsibilities and human judgment.
- Knowledge management: connecting information, reusable experience, and action.
- Gamification and interaction design that make complex tools more approachable.
- Open-source experiments that others can understand, reproduce, and improve.

Owen co-founded Robinson as a place for people with different backgrounds to learn and build together. He welcomes concrete problems, thoughtful feedback, and collaborations that connect research with practice.
